Overview

Who We Are

Ethos Empire LLC, an Idaho limited liability company ("Ethos Empire", "we", "us", or "our"), operates the website located at ethosempire.org. We are a personal growth and self-mastery brand that sells digital ebooks and physical merchandise through third-party platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected in connection with your visit to our site, how it is used, and what your rights are. It reflects the site as it actually operates today — a static website with no accounts, no checkout, no analytics scripts, and no first-party tracking.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not run our own analytics. We do not maintain an email list.

Section 1

Information We Collect

Information we collect directly (first-party)

The ethosempire.org site itself is a static website. It has no user accounts, no login system, no checkout, no contact forms, no newsletter signup, and no analytics or tracking scripts. The only information the site stores in your browser is:

  • One localStorage preference (key ee_cookie_consent) that records your choice on the cookie consent banner (see Section 4). It stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Information our hosting provider may log

The website is served from a third-party static-hosting provider. Standard server-access data — such as your IP address, request timestamp, requested URL, referrer, and user-agent string — may be logged by that provider for security, abuse prevention, and uptime purposes. Ethos Empire does not receive or process those raw server logs.

Information handled by third-party platforms

Purchases are processed entirely by Gumroad (ebooks) and Printful (physical merchandise). We do not see, store, or handle your payment card details, billing address, or order details — those are collected and processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies (see Section 3).

Direct correspondence

If you contact us by email or social media, we retain that correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and to address any follow-up.

Section 2

How We Use Your Information

The data collected from site visits is used only to:

  • Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • Monitor site performance, uptime, and security
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data to send unsolicited emails, build advertising profiles, or share it with data brokers. We have no email newsletter or mailing list at this time.

Section 3

Third-Party Services

Our website integrates or links to the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Gumroad — processes all ebook purchases and manages digital delivery. Gumroad Privacy Policy
  • Printful — fulfils and ships all physical merchandise orders. Printful Privacy Policy
  • Social media platforms — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, Discord, LinkedIn, Snapchat, and Pinterest. When you click through to these platforms, their respective privacy policies apply.
  • Hosting / CDN providers — our hosting infrastructure may log standard server access data (IP address, request timestamps) for security and uptime monitoring.

Embedded content or links from these platforms may load third-party cookies or pixels in your browser. We do not control this and recommend reviewing each platform's privacy policy.

Section 4

Cookies & Local Storage

What ethosempire.org actually stores in your browser

The Ethos Empire website does not set first-party analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels. The only browser-side storage it uses is:

  • ee_cookie_consent — a single localStorage entry that remembers your response to the consent banner (either all or essential). It stays on your device.

The cookie consent banner

When you first visit the site, a banner appears at the bottom of the page. Because we do not currently run first-party analytics or advertising trackers, both options function primarily to record your preference:

  • Accept All — records your acceptance in case optional analytics or preference features are added in the future.
  • Essential Only — records that you decline any future non-essential storage.

If we later add optional analytics or similar features, we will honour your recorded preference before loading them. You can change your choice at any time by clearing localStorage for ethosempire.org in your browser and revisiting the site.

Third-party cookies

Third-party services you reach from this site — Gumroad, Printful, social platforms — set their own cookies once you visit them. Those are governed by their own privacy policies (see Section 3). Our site does not embed remote scripts, pixels, or iframes from those services on ethosempire.org itself.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent you from reading content on our site or accessing our external store links.

Section 5

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data including:

  • The right to know what personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal data (we do not sell data)
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to correct inaccurate information

Because we do not collect personal information directly (purchases go through Gumroad and Printful), most data-related requests should be directed to those platforms. For any request related to data you believe we hold, contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

Section 6

Our website and products are intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove it.

Section 7

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to a static website of this nature. Because the site has no accounts, no checkout, and no first-party data store, there is no user database on our side to protect — payment card information, billing details, and order records are handled by Gumroad and Printful under their own security programmes.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure. We do not represent or warrant that any communication with us is impervious to interception or compromise, and we will notify affected individuals of any confirmed security incident to the extent required by applicable law.
